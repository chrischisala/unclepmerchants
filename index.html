Behind the Legend: Inspector Point Mbewe

In Zambia, few names command as much cultural respect, viral energy, and automotive reverence as Point Mbewe—popularly celebrated across the nation and southern Africa as Uncle P.

Holding the rank of Inspector in the Zambia Police Service, Point Mbewe defies conventions by harmoniously balancing a distinguished career upholding law and order with running Zambia's most innovative automotive styling atelier. His dedication, discipline, and infectious energy have propelled Uncle P Merchants into a nationwide cultural movement.

"Merchant mwine! Basakayese! When we build a car, we are putting Zambian engineering on the world stage."

— Point Mbewe, Founder & Lead Visionary

From the bustling streets of Kamwala South in Lusaka to premier motorsport gatherings, Uncle P's distinct styling signature—daring chameleon wraps, air-ride drops, bespoke leather cabins, and custom aerodynamics—has set the benchmark for dealerships and car enthusiasts throughout Zambia.
